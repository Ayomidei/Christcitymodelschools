Welcome to CCMS

A Heritage of Excellence & Discipline

At Christ City Model Schools, we provide an all-round learning experience tailored to every child's growth stage. From Creche and Nursery to Primary and Secondary levels, we blend modern teaching with foundational moral values.

Our academic curriculum is enriched with practical science, technology, leadership development, and active competitive sports.

Our Academic Spectrum

Learning Tailored To Every Growth Stage

Explore our four distinct academic tiers designed for holistic development.

Creche (3 Months – 2 Years)

A safe, hygienic, and nurturing home-away-from-home where infants receive personalized care, sensory stimulation, and early motor skill development.

  • 24/7 Monitored Safe Environment
  • Sensory & Cognitive Play Equipment
  • Experienced Early Care Givers
Enroll in Creche

Nursery Education (Ages 3 – 5)

Building phonics, early numeracy, social etiquette, and confidence through activity-based play and structured foundational instruction.

  • Phonics & Early Reading Mastery
  • Creative Art & Music Sessions
  • Social Interaction & Manners
Enroll in Nursery

Primary School (Basic 1 – 6)

Fostering critical thinking, STEM foundations, language proficiency, and sportsmanship to prepare pupils for junior secondary entrance exams.

  • Comprehensive National Curriculum
  • Computer Studies & Coding Fundamentals
  • Inter-House Sports Competitions
Enroll in Primary

Secondary School (JSS1 – SSS3)

Rigorous science, arts, and commercial pathways equipped with modern laboratories, leadership workshops, and WAEC/NECO coaching.

  • Fully Equipped Science & ICT Labs
  • Career Guidance & Leadership Seminars
  • Excellence in Regional Sports Leagues
Enroll in Secondary
