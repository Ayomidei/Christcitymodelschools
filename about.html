About Our School

Discovering Potential, Molding Character, Building Leaders

Our Foundation

Our History & Heritage

Christ City Model Schools was founded on a vision to deliver exceptional, affordable, and holistic education. Starting with a vision to nurture young minds from the foundational Creche level, the institution grew into a full academic ecosystem spanning Nursery, Primary, and Secondary education.

Our commitment remains unchanged: providing a safe, welcoming, and disciplined environment where children excel academically, morally, and physically.

Our Mission

To Operate A Transparent And Efficient Educational System That Optimizes Mental and Physical Development of A Child

Our Vision

To Deliver Quality Educational Services To Students In partnership with other Stakeholders

Guiding Principles

Our Core Values

Academic Rigor

Promoting curiosity, high performance, and critical thinking across all grade levels.

Moral Discipline

Instilling integrity, respect, empathy, and strong moral grounding in daily student life.

Athletic Spirit

Fostering physical wellness, teamwork, resilience, and sportsmanship through organized athletics.

Innovation & STEM

Integrating modern digital tools, coding fundamentals, and practical science into early learning.

Sports & Extracurricular Excellence

At Christ City Model Schools, athletic development goes hand-in-hand with academic learning. Our sports curriculum includes football leagues, track & field events, basketball, table tennis, and physical fitness coaching across all levels.
